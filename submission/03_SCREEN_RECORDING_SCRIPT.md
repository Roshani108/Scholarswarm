#### 🎬 Part 1: The Problem & Meet Rahul (0:00 - 0:45)
* **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Open ScholarSwarm. Show the top navigation bar and the **Active Candidate** bar.
  - Hover over Rahul Sharma's profile: `8.2 CGPA`, `NIT Jalandhar`, `Target: Canada, Germany, Singapore`.
* **What to Say:**
  > "Hello everyone! Meet Rahul Sharma. He is a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student from NIT Jalandhar with an 8.2 CGPA. Rahul has strong AI projects on his GitHub, but his family cannot afford expensive tuition fees abroad. He dreams of studying in Canada, Germany, or Singapore on a full scholarship, but he doesn't know where he qualifies, how to apply, or what deadlines exist.
  > 
  > To solve this, we built **ScholarSwarm** — an autonomous multi-agent AI system that acts as Rahul's personal admissions committee and scholarship strategist."

---

#### 🎬 Part 2: Launching the AI Swarm (0:45 - 1:45)
* **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Click the green **`Run AI Swarm Analysis`** button.
  - Show the 4-step execution pipeline lighting up dynamically:
    1. `Profile Ingestion Agent` (reading transcript, parsing GitHub)
    2. `Opportunity Discovery Agent` (scanning university portals)
    3. `Eligibility Validation Agent` (checking 8.2 CGPA cutoffs)
    4. `Strategist & Ranking Agent` (calculating zero-debt match scores)
* **What to Say:**
  > "When I click 'Run AI Swarm Analysis', four autonomous AI agents spring into action. 
  > First, the Profile Ingestion Agent parses Rahul's marksheet and public GitHub repositories. 
  > Second, the Opportunity Discovery Agent scans accredited universities across Canada, Germany, Singapore, and the UK.
  > Third, the Eligibility Validator strictly checks minimum CGPA and language requirements so Rahul doesn't waste time on programs he isn't eligible for.
  > Finally, the Strategist Ranker calculates a Composite Match Score prioritizing zero-debt tuition waivers and research stipends."

---

#### 🎬 Part 3: The Top 10 University Results (1:45 - 2:45)
* **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Scroll smoothly through the clean 3-column campus photo cards.
  - Point out:
    - **TUM Munich (Germany)**: 98% Match, €0 Tuition, DAAD Scholarship.
    - **University of Toronto (Canada)**: 94% Match, Vector Institute AI Fellowship ($35k/yr).
    - **National University of Singapore (NUS)**: 91% Match, NUS Graduate School Scholarship (Full Tuition + S$2,200/mo).
    - Direct **`Apply on Official Website ↗`** portal buttons.
* **What to Say:**
  > "Here are Rahul's Top 10 curated opportunities, each complete with real campus pictures and clear funding tags. 
  > For instance, at Technical University of Munich, tuition is zero, and Rahul qualifies for the DAAD Research Fellowship.
  > At University of Toronto, his AI background matches the Vector Institute AI Fellowship, providing a $35,000 yearly stipend.
  > Most importantly, every single card has a direct link to the official university portal so Rahul can apply immediately without middlemen."

---

#### 🎬 Part 4: Zero-Hallucination SOP & LOR Studio (2:45 - 3:45)
* **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Click the **`SOP & LOR Studio`** tab.
  - Highlight the generated Statement of Purpose: show the highlighted citations linking to Rahul's specific GitHub repo (`@rahul-cse/autonomous-agent`) and his 8.2 CGPA transcript.
* **What to Say:**
  > "One of our most critical features is the Statement of Purpose and LOR generator. 
  > Unlike standard LLMs that invent fictional achievements, ScholarSwarm enforces a strict **Zero-Hallucination Rule**: every paragraph cites verified evidence directly from Rahul's verified transcripts and GitHub commits. 
  > Admissions committees can verify every single claim."

---

#### 🎬 Part 5: Deadline Calendar, PDF Export & Conclusion (3:45 - 5:00)
* **What to Show on Screen:**
  - Click on the **`Deadline Calendar`** tab (shows countdowns and priority badges).
  - Click the **`Save PDF`** button in the header (shows clean, print-ready admissions dossier).
  - Switch to Ananya or Priya in the candidate bar to show the system works for any student.
* **What to Say:**
  > "Rahul can also switch to the Deadline Calendar to see upcoming application cutoffs organized chronologically so he never misses a deadline. 
  > With one click on 'Save PDF', he can download or print his complete scholarship dossier.
  > And ScholarSwarm isn't just for Rahul — any student can upload their own resume or switch candidates with one click.
  > 
  > ScholarSwarm transforms weeks of stressful scholarship searching into 30 seconds of clear, autonomous guidance. Thank you!"
